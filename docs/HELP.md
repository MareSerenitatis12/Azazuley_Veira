# Azazuley Veira

> A dark room listens.
> Breath enters without a name.
> Many tongues awaken.

Azazuley Veira is a terminal-shaped application for the bundled TardiSHA GrimChain and Sydonic Magicae bodies. It is not a general operating-system shell.

## Et Sonyera>

> A word enters night.
> Something beneath it answers.
> The hidden song wakes.

**Et Sonyera** means **the Song Within**.

Enter source text here and press Enter to send the exact text through bundled TardiSHA GrimChain. If `Domus Count>` contains a value, that value is the requested visible depth.

Normally the requested depth is the final GrimChain depth. In the rare case that the finite body contains only Enochian grammar and no Real speaking body, Sydonic continues the same witnessed GrimChain one coordinate at a time until the first speaking body exists. The entire resulting GrimChain becomes the visible, rendered, translated, and journaled body. Nothing from the opening body is hidden or shifted.

The font selector sits on the Et Sonyera label row. It changes only Et Sonyera. A selection changes the selected text; otherwise the chosen font becomes the current format for typing from the cursor.

## GrimChain>

> An old path returns.
> The door knows the shape of it.
> The room speaks again.

Paste an existing complete GrimChain here and press Enter.

Azazuley requires TardiSHA's current public Living Domus structure before the body is accepted. This is structural recognition of the public GrimChain. It is not source-file recomputation or manifest verification.

A complete GrimChain submitted here enters the direct translation path. It is not regenerated or unfolded again merely because Enochian bearings appear near an edge. Existing Aeternum law resolves those bearings through the complete finite body whenever a Real speaking body exists.

Malformed input is rejected before Sydonic rendering.

## Domus Count>

> Rain gathers in stone.
> The spring keeps its hidden course.
> A vessel fills.

Enter the requested visible Domus Middle value used when Et Sonyera creates a new body.

Submitting Domus Count while Et Sonyera contains text re-submits the current song through GrimChain with that count. If the rare all-Enochian condition requires lawful continuation, the final visible depth may extend beyond the requested count until the first Real speaking body appears.

The same current value is used when Azazuley creates the GrimChain for exported `.shk` bodies and the PDF self-return for exported PDFs.

## Domus House>

> Four windows hold light.
> Each face keeps the same fire.
> The house does not speak twice.

**Domus House** is the visible mirror presentation of the accepted GrimChain.

The house shows four exact 12-glyph fields from the accepted GrimChain:

- left reflection: reverse of the first 12 glyphs;
- upper center: the first 12 glyphs;
- lower center: the last 12 glyphs;
- right reflection: reverse of the last 12 glyphs.

The four fields use exact mixed-face GrimChain rendering and fit from the actual rendered glyph metrics. They are presentation only. Rabulalia, Ailalubar, and Sydonic receive the complete accepted GrimChain.

## Output tabs

> One flame, many rooms.
> Each room receives its own light.
> None steals another voice.

**Rabulalia** is **the Cosmic Babble of Creation**. It displays the complete accepted/generated GrimChain.

**Ailalubar** is the mirrored utterance of Rabulalia — the Cosmic Babble of Creation. It presents that mirrored utterance through the exact visual witness carried by the current rendered body.

### Prosody and Cantillation

> A river has two faces.
> One remembers every stone.
> One shines beneath the moon.

**Prosody** is the underlying Unicode/code-point order and its source coordinates. Mixed right-to-left and left-to-right writing systems can cause the natural visual order presented on screen to differ from that stored order.

**Cantillation** is the deterministic natural visual character sequence presented to the reader through Ailalubar.

Sydonic translates Cantillation: the same natural visual character sequence a human encounters in Ailalubar. It does not substitute the hidden Prosody/code-point order as translation order. The visual sequence retains an exact mapping back to the original Prosody source coordinates so translation provenance remains auditable.

Fonts and glyphs are never artificially reversed, flipped, or replaced to manufacture Cantillation. Ailalubar does not substitute mirrored Unicode counterparts or invent per-font direction rules.

**Azalalia** is **the Language Damons speak**. It displays the forward authored written-word projection, with the 𑁦 breath separator between resolved words.

**Ailalaza** displays the reverse projection of those same resolved authored words. It does not perform a second translation.

**Glossolalia** displays the forward pronunciation projection from the resolved lexical bodies.

**Ailalossolg** displays the mirrored pronunciation projection from those same resolved lexical bodies.

**LeySyff | FfysYel** is one paired scripture tab containing two independent exact text surfaces. LeySyff is the forward Ostensive projection on the left. FfysYel is the mirrored Ostensive projection on the right. Each side independently selects complete authored Ostensive utterances, groups them into paragraphs of 1–6 complete utterances, and chooses one Hebrew paragraph-prefix character from the authored presentation pool. The two sides share one Terminal-owned physical page geometry and one vertical scrollbar while keeping independent paragraph boundaries, page counts, prefixes, and text lengths. Paragraphs remain atomic: a complete paragraph moves intact to the next leaf when it does not fit.

LeySyff is forced left-to-right and left aligned. FfysYel uses natural bidirectional layout and right alignment so each Hebrew paragraph prefix appears at the visual beginning of its paragraph on the right. The two exact boxes touch directly and form one center seam; there is no inserted divider character in either text body.

An explicitly present empty `.aksh` field is valid authored data. Its runtime value remains the empty string and the corresponding presentation remains empty. Only structural absence of a required field name is an authority failure.

**Definition** displays the structured authored lexical definition view.

**Canon Glossary** is the static dictionary reference shown after Definition. It reads only `aksh/Dictionary.aksh`, which contains the canon glossary and glyph-dictionary material from `ALQC_GLOSSARY.md` and `DICTIONARY.md`, plus Regia, Breath, Cantillation, and Prosody. `DICTIONARY_OF_SYMBOLS.md` is not part of this tab. The glossary does not participate in GrimChain generation, Sydonic translation, or render transactions.

All engine output fields are read-only but remain selectable and copyable.

## Font selector

The first item is **System Default Font**. The remaining choices are families available through Qt after the installed `/usr/local/share/fonts/azazuley` and `/usr/local/share/fonts/tardisha` runtime offices have loaded.

Engine-facing GrimChain glyph fields use the exact mapped TardiSHA physical font file. Azalalia and Ailalaza apply each authored lexical span from its exact `.aksh` font file and source authority.

## GrimChain History

> Old footprints remain.
> The path walked again comes first.
> Snow keeps only one trail.

The dropdown at the upper left shows retained GrimChains from the local journal, newest first. The journal lives under `~/.grimchain/azazuley/grimchains/` and consists of the active `grimchains.bio` plus up to three compressed rolling `.bio` backups. The active journal rotates at 32 MiB.

History is keyed by the exact GrimChain body. A chain appears once in the visible history. A newly encountered chain is added at the newest position. When an existing chain is translated again or imported again, Azazuley promotes its existing record to the newest position instead of writing another duplicate. Its associated `User input` is preserved and its recency timestamp is refreshed. Promotion can move a chain out of a rotated compressed generation back into the active journal. Older duplicate records left by previous append-only versions are suppressed in the history view and consolidated when reused.

Opening the dropdown starts a temporary history-reading session. It loads 40 unique records at a time and fetches the next 40 as you continue scrolling. It keeps the current read state while the dropdown is in use instead of rereading the same journal data. Older compressed generations are opened only if scrolling reaches them.

Selecting an entry places that exact GrimChain into `GrimChain>` and does not translate it merely because it was selected. The temporary history cache is then discarded. If the user submits the loaded chain, the complete stored body translates directly and that same history identity moves to the newest position.

## Import

> A sealed road arrives.
> Familiar steps move forward;
> new ones join the path.

IMPORT accepts `.grim` manifest files. Each manifest entry that contains a GrimChain is validated and written to local history using that entry's stored path as `User input`. Directory entries or other manifest records without a GrimChain do not create journal records. The standalone GrimChain at the end of the `.grim` belongs to the manifest itself and is recorded with the `.grim` filename as `User input`.

Because `.grim` files contain no journal timestamp, a new history entry receives the time the import is logged. All GrimChains from the selected import are validated before any of them are committed. If an imported exact GrimChain already exists, the existing record is promoted rather than duplicated. Importing a manifest does not render each imported chain through the translation viewer.

## Export

> A page crosses the gate.
> Its echo travels beneath it.
> The whole song leaves whole.

EXPORT writes to `~/.grimchain/azazuley/exports/` and provides three actions.

**Export GrimChained Bio (.shk)** exports the active unarchived `grimchains.bio` as `grimchains.shk`. The live `.bio` is not modified. The exported file contains the exact active `.bio` bytes followed by one final newline whose only content is the GrimChain of that `.bio` at the current `Domus Count>`.

**Export Words & Definitions (PDF)** exports the complete current rendered translation in this order:

1. Rabulalia
2. Ailalubar
3. Azalalia
4. Ailalaza
5. Glossolalia
6. Ailalossolg
7. `LeySyff/FfysYel`
8. Definition

Each section begins on a fresh PDF page and is framed with the literal Azazuley binding glyph:

```text
⧟⧟⧟⧟⧟⧟⧟⧟⧟⧟⧟⧟⧟⧟⧟⧟⧟⧟⧟⧟⧟⧟⧟⧟⧟⧟⧟
Rabulalia
⧟⧟⧟⧟⧟⧟⧟⧟⧟⧟⧟⧟⧟⧟⧟⧟⧟⧟⧟⧟⧟⧟⧟⧟⧟⧟⧟

<VALUE>
```

The same framing is used for every translation section. `LeySyff/FfysYel` remains one paired rendered section rather than being flattened into a replacement text body.

**Export Canon Glossary (PDF)** exports Canon Glossary separately and uses the same section framing.

Both PDFs are painted from the existing exact rendered surfaces and are then completed through TardiSHA's public PDF self-return using the current user `Domus Count>`. The embedded GrimChain is verified before the completed PDF replaces the unsealed export.

Read-only output fields remain selectable and copyable, so text may still be copied into a user's own `.txt`, `.md`, `.odt`, or other document. Those are manual copies rather than Azazuley export formats.
